Data source

At 60× one real second is one minute.

The demo creates realistic readings for a right leg cast applied two days ago, including one past heel episode. All values are simulated.

Connects to the sensing sock over Bluetooth Low Energy (Nordic UART service) and reads one JSON line per sample.

Device data format
JSON lines, one per sample, about 1 Hz (slower is fine). Pressure as raw 12-bit ADC (0–4095), temperature in °C, humidity in % RH, acceleration in g. Sensor order: heel medial, heel lateral, medial malleolus, lateral malleolus, instep, 5th metatarsal head, navicular/cuboid. 
{"t":1731160000,"p":[812,903,455,610,380,520,470],"T":[32.6,32.9,33.4,33.6,33.9,33.1,33.3],"rh":74.2,"a":[0.01,-0.98,0.05]}
CSV with a header row works too. Time can be epoch seconds or ISO 8601: 
time,p1,p2,p3,p4,p5,p6,p7,T1,T2,T3,T4,T5,T6,T7,rh,ax,ay,az

Alert rules

These limits are design assumptions for the prototype, not clinically validated values. Changes apply to all readings, including past ones.

Pressure calibration

FSRs are non-linear. The app converts raw ADC counts to mmHg with this table (linear between points). Build it by loading a sensor with known weights through a flat disc of known area. In demo mode, raw values are calculated back from the simulated pressure.

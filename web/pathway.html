SmartCast Care Pathway
SmartCast · MTI project

From sensor reading to clinical intervention

How a pressure problem under the cast is detected, acted on at home, and escalated to the treating clinic. It works whether or not the patient can report pain themselves.

Automatic, device or app Done by a person Decision Alert Emergency

Step by step

#StepWho
1–3The clinic applies the cast over the sensing sock. The ESP32 reads 7 pressure and 7 temperature points, humidity and movement about once per second and sends them over Bluetooth.Automatic
4–6The app converts raw ADC counts to mmHg, drops readings taken during movement and short spikes, and updates the 3D heatmap and history.Automatic
7–8If a rule is met, the phone shows a warning or critical alert with concrete steps.Automatic
A–BPain or distress can be logged at any time with one tap. For patients who cannot speak, crying and restlessness are what the caregiver logs.Caregiver
9–10The caregiver repositions and elevates the leg. The app checks whether pressure drops and the episode resolves.Caregiver App
11–12If not, the app builds a PDF and CSV report, which the caregiver emails to the clinic or brings up on the phone call.App Caregiver
13–14The doctor sees where the hotspot is, how long it lasted and what the caregiver noticed, and decides: advice, cast check (window, split, re-pad, replace) or emergency care.Doctor

Alert rules at step 7

SignalRule
PressureAbove 40 mmHg for 10 min gives a warning, for 30 min a critical alert
Skin temperatureAbove 38 °C for 5 min, or one spot 2 °C warmer than the rest for 20 min
HumidityAbove 90 % RH for 30 min (cast may be wet)
SwellingAverage pressure 8 mmHg higher than 6 h earlier
Data lossNo readings from a sensor for 2 min

All limits are design assumptions for the prototype and can be changed in the app. They still need clinical validation.

In the prototype, sending the report is a manual step (email or phone). A direct link to the clinic would be a later version.
